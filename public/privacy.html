Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

1. What We Collect

Love Note does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any third-party server. Everything happens inside your browser:

  • Recipient lists — Parsed and held in your browser's memory only. Never uploaded anywhere.
  • Email content — Composed and previewed locally. Sent directly from your browser to Google's Gmail API.
  • Authentication — You sign in via Google OAuth. We receive an access token that lives only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session.

2. What We Don't Collect

  • We do not read, access, or scan your Gmail inbox.
  • We do not store your emails, contacts, or messages on any server.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not share or sell any data — we have no data to share.

3. Google OAuth Scopes

We request the following permissions from your Google account:

  • gmail.send — To send emails from your account on your behalf.
  • userinfo.email — To know which email address the emails are being sent from.
  • userinfo.profile — To display your name in the app.
  • openid — To associate your Google account with your session.

These are the absolute minimum scopes needed to function. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

4. Data Flow

When you click "Send":

  1. Your browser builds an RFC 2822 email message locally.
  2. The message is sent directly to gmail.googleapis.com via HTTPS.
  3. The recipient's email appears in your Gmail Sent folder.
  4. No copy of the message or recipient list is retained by Love Note.
