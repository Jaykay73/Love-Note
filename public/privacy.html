Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

About Love Note

Love Note is a browser-based tool that lets church staff and volunteers send personalized batch emails using their own Gmail account. The entire application runs in your browser — we have no backend servers and store none of your data.

1. What We Collect

Love Note does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any third-party server. Everything happens inside your browser:

  • Recipient lists — Parsed and held in your browser's memory only. Never uploaded anywhere.
  • Email content — Composed and previewed locally. Sent directly from your browser to Google's Gmail API.
  • Authentication — You sign in via Google OAuth. We receive an access token that lives only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session.

2. What We Don't Collect

  • We do not read, access, or scan your Gmail inbox.
  • We do not store your emails, contacts, or messages on any server.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not share or sell any data — we have no data to share.

3. Google OAuth Scopes and Why We Need Them

To function, Love Note needs the following permissions from your Google account. Each is strictly necessary:

  • gmail.send — Required to send emails from your Gmail address to your recipients. Without this scope the app cannot deliver any messages. We never read, delete, or modify any existing emails in your account.
  • userinfo.email — Required to know your Gmail address so emails appear to come from you (the "From" field). Without this we cannot construct a valid email message.
  • userinfo.profile — Used only to display your name in the app header so you know which account you're signed into. Not required for sending.
  • openid — Required by Google's authentication system to establish a secure sign-in session. This is a standard OpenID Connect scope.

You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

4. Data Flow

When you click "Send":

  1. Your browser builds an RFC 2822 email message locally.
  2. The message is sent directly to gmail.googleapis.com via HTTPS.
  3. The recipient's email appears in your Gmail Sent folder.
  4. No copy of the message or recipient list is retained by Love Note.
